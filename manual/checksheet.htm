IV. TRs PRACTICAL

Do each drill to a pass following coaching instructions and other rules laid down.
Go back and redo earlier drills when needed. Restudy instructions before each drill as needed. TR-0 has to be done until the student can do it for 2 hours straight without flunking or interruptions. This may take a number of attempts. It may work best to set targets of doing it for 15 or 30 minutes and gradually increase the time until the 2 hours drill is passed. There are usually a number of TR Remedies applied on this step, such as mood drills, etc. They are not listed as they are only done as needed, but they count full student points.
